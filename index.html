JARVIS TRADER

Rules-based gold trading assistant for MetaTrader 5. It runs on your own PC with your own account, and you control it from your phone.

Download for Windows

Free installer, one file, version 2.55.7. Windows 10 or 11.

Three steps

  1. Download and open it. It sets everything up by itself in about 5 minutes and puts a "Jarvis Trader" icon on your desktop.
  2. Create your account on the screen that opens, then follow the Setup tab. Use a demo account at your broker first.
  3. Updates are automatic. You never download it again.

On your phone

  1. Install the free Tailscale app on your PC and your phone, signed in to the same account.
  2. In Jarvis on your PC: Setup → Phone access → switch on.
  3. Open the link it shows on your phone and sign in. Keep the PC and MetaTrader 5 on.

Your phone connects privately to your own PC. Nobody can reach anyone else's bot.
